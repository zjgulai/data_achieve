# Style notes and rationale

## Retained from the current product

- Warm paper canvas and white operational surfaces.
- Rose identity and existing Lucide icon set.
- Six-entry information architecture.
- Shared Workbench primitives, ActionGate and Project selector.
- Explicit read-only / preview / no-live-call boundaries.

## Changed in the stable system

- Rose is a scarce brand/action color, not a universal state or border color.
- Status colors are semantic and accessible; labels and explanations remain mandatory.
- Radius is reduced and hierarchical; pills are limited to status and filters.
- Default panels lose decorative shadow and repeated colored left strips.
- Business content and diagnostics are separated through progressive disclosure.
- Data comparison shifts from repeated cards toward tables, rows and split-pane workspaces.

## Explicitly rejected

- Reference-brand copying.
- Purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism and glow effects.
- Dark mode as a prerequisite for this iteration.
- New icon or font dependencies.
- A big-bang rewrite of existing components or 267 color values.
